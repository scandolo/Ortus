Your browser closes soon

The Ortus extension is off, so your blocked websites aren't blocked. Until focus ends, Ortus closes this browser. If you reopen it, the countdown starts again.

Keep it open

  1. Click the Ortus icon in your menu bar, then Fix.
  2. Turn the Ortus extension on.
  3. Open its Details and switch on Allow in Incognito.

Is the extension already on? Report a bug.
