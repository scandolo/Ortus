Your browser closes soon

The Ortus extension is off, removed or not allowed in incognito, so your blocked websites aren't fully blocked. Until focus ends, Ortus closes this browser. If you reopen it, the countdown starts again.

Keep it open

  1. Click the Ortus icon in your menu bar, then Fix.
  2. Turn the Ortus extension back on, or add it again if you removed it.
  3. Open its Details and turn on Allow in Incognito.

Is the extension already on? Report a bug.

Turn on Allow in Incognito

Three steps. Once it's on, this tab closes by itself.

Does steps 1 and 2 for you.

  1. 1Open chrome://extensions

    Websites can't link there, so paste it in the address bar.

    chrome://extensions

  2. 2Find Ortus · Website blocking and click Details

  3. 3Scroll down to Allow in Incognito and turn it on

    It's below Pin to toolbar.

Is it already on? Report a bug.
