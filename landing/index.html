If you need to stop

Not yet.
Locked until 11:32.

48 minutes to go. Before the first session, Ortus shows what will close, when it ends, and the one way out. You confirm with Understood, start.

  1. What is blocked.

    The apps and the sites in the mode. Reopen one and it closes again. Ortus, Finder and System Settings stay available.

  2. How long.

    Until the time you set. A schedule ends when its window ends, and a scheduled session has no cancel window.

  3. The one way out.

    Changed your mind? You have 30 seconds. The button says Never mind. After that, End focus early, in Settings, once a calendar week. That weekly end reopens the apps Ortus closed. When the timer finishes on its own, the sites load again. Closed apps stay closed until you open them.

The details

Questions.

What if something urgent happens?

Almost nothing is so urgent it cannot wait until the session ends. The fear is real. The urgency usually is not. Ask your agent and you get that answer, not the rest of the inbox. If it truly cannot wait, End focus early is in Settings, once a week. Ortus does not watch for urgent messages on its own.

What if I need Slack or email to do the work?

Ask your agent for the message or the file. It runs the Claude Code already on your Mac, with the tools you connected there. The app stays closed. That is a short step. Opening Slack is how the morning goes.

What if I really need to stop?

A session you start gives you 30 seconds. The button says Never mind. After that, the session holds. The remaining way out is End focus early, in Settings, once a calendar week. A scheduled session has no cancel window. That weekly end reopens the apps Ortus closed. When the timer ends on its own, websites load again. Closed apps stay closed until you open them.

What about my phone?

Ortus guards the Mac. It does not block the phone. Put the phone in another room. iPhone support is on the roadmap, and it is not available now.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Ortus is free. There is no account and no trial to expire. The license is MIT.

Where does my data go?

Settings stay on your Mac. On signed releases, a Slack token is stored in the macOS login Keychain and is not synced to iCloud. Ortus sends that token only to Slack. The extension talks only to the app on your Mac and sends nothing over the internet. Chat goes to Anthropic under your own Claude account, not to us. The privacy policy has the rest.

What if I do not have Claude Code?

The blocker works without it. Chat is the part that needs your own Claude Code. Ortus looks for it on your Mac. Settings, then Chat, then Configure, if it lives somewhere else.

Which browsers can block websites?

Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave, on Chromium 120 or later. Safari and Firefox are not supported. After you add the extension, open its Details and turn on Allow in Incognito. Mac apps close without the extension. Websites need it.

What does a session block?

Socials blocks LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit and TikTok. Messages blocks Gmail, Slack and WhatsApp. Everything blocks both. A custom mode is your own sites and Mac apps. Websites include their subdomains. Gmail means mail.google.com and gmail.com, so the rest of Google stays open. Ortus, Finder and System Settings stay available.

Can I quit during a session?

No. Cmd+Q, the Dock and a programmatic quit fail while a session is running. Quitting is not the way out.
