Why we built Ortus

Distraction is the default now. You open Slack, X or email on muscle memory, for one quick check. Blink, and the morning is gone. The one thing that mattered hasn't moved.

Every fix we tried was too soft or too dumb. Do Not Disturb, Focus modes and Slack statuses mute the noise but leave the app one click away, and almost half of all interruptions are ones we start ourselves.1 Hard blockers do hold, but they also lock you out of the message or file you need to get the work done, so they end up switched off.

So we built one that holds, and still lets you work.

1. Dabbish, Mark and González, “Why Do I Keep Interrupting Myself?”, CHI 2011: almost half of the interruptions people experience are self-initiated.

How it works

Set it once.Then it holds.

  1. 1

    Pick what pulls you away.

    Slack, X, email, YouTube, or any app or website you choose.

  2. 2

    Start now, or on a schedule.

    One session when you need it, or every weekday morning, automatically.

  3. 3

    The wall holds.

    There's no end button, only one emergency exit a week.

Your agent

Ask.Don't open.

During a session, ask Ortus for the message, thread or file you need. Your own Claude Code reads whatever you've connected to it, like Slack, Gmail or your calendar, and answers right inside Ortus. You get what you came for, and none of the feed around it.

Uses your own Claude Code. The blocker works fully without it.

Before you lock in

Questions?

What if something urgent happens?

Almost nothing is so urgent it can't wait two hours. If you need to check something, ask your agent. And Settings has one emergency end per calendar week.

Is no end button too extreme?

That's the point: if you could stop it, you would. When you start a session, you get a short grace period to change your mind, then it's locked. Scheduled sessions lock right away.

Which apps and websites can I block?

Any app on your Mac. Websites are blocked with a small browser extension for Chrome and other Chromium browsers: Arc, Dia, Comet, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Opera.

Do I need Claude Code?

No, the blocker works fully without it. For now, chat runs on your own Claude Code, so AI inside Ortus costs you nothing extra. More ways to connect are coming.

What about my phone?

Ortus guards your Mac, where the work happens. Put the phone in another room. iPhone is on the roadmap.

Does Ortus read my messages?

It runs on your Mac with no account and no server of ours. When you ask a question, your own Claude Code does the reading.

How much does Ortus cost?

Nothing. It's free and open source, and there's no account to create.
