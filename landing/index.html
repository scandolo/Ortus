Before you start

The rules.

Said once, with your own apps and your own end time, the first time you lock in.

  1. No end button.It is not on the panel. There is nothing handy to talk yourself into.
  2. Quitting is blocked.Cmd+Q, the Dock, a script. The whole session, including the first 30 seconds.
  3. 30 seconds, if you started it.Never mind, or skip and lock in now. A schedule does not give you those 30 seconds.
  4. One early end a week.In Settings. It unlocks everything and reopens what Ortus closed. Then it is gone until next week.
  5. Add 15 minutes.You can make a session longer. You cannot make it shorter.
  6. Apps stay closed.When time is up, websites load again. Apps Ortus quit stay quit, unless you used the early end.
  7. Modes.Socials, Messages, Everything, or a custom list of sites and Mac apps. Websites include their subdomains.
  8. The extension.Mac apps close on their own. Websites need Ortus · Website blocking in Chrome, Arc, Edge or Brave, allowed in Incognito.
  9. Fails open.If Ortus stops publishing a heartbeat, the website rules drop within 20 seconds.
  10. No account.Usage stats are opt-in and anonymous. On the signed app, Slack's token stays in the login keychain, off iCloud.
  11. Free.MIT. Signed and notarized. macOS 14 or later.

Questions

Almost nothing is urgent.

What if something actually is?

Ask your agent. It can check Slack or Gmail while they stay closed, and bring back the message or the file. A real emergency is End focus early, once a week, in Settings.

What about my phone?

Ortus locks the Mac, which is where the work is. It closes the Slack app, the browser and the sites you chose. It does not lock your phone.

I need something from Slack to do the work.

Ask. Chat uses the Claude Code already installed on your Mac, and the Slack or Gmail tools you connected there. A teammate's message or a PDF, without the app pulling you in. Five seconds to a minute, and the session holds.

Can I quit the app?

While a session is running, no. That includes the 30 second window. Never mind is the way out of a session you just started. After that, the early end in Settings is the one door, once a week.

What does chat send, and to whom?

Your questions go from Claude Code to Anthropic under your own Claude account. Ortus does not receive them and does not keep them after you quit. The extension talks only to the Ortus app on your Mac. There is no Ortus account. Anonymous usage events are off unless you opt in. Privacy policy.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Free and open source, MIT. Download the signed app, or build it from the repo.
