01 · The session

Once it starts, it holds.

The focus app with no off switch. A schedule or a timer. Then the door stays shut.

  1. 1

    Start it

    Put it on a schedule, like weekday mornings until noon, or start a timer from 15 minutes to 4 hours. The first time, Ortus shows what will close, when it ends, and the one way out.

  2. 2

    It closes them

    Mac apps quit. Websites in Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave hit a wall. Reopen a blocked app and Ortus closes it again. Ortus, Finder and System Settings stay open.

  3. 3

    No end button

    Start one yourself and you get 30 seconds to press Never mind. After that, and for every session a schedule starts, it runs to the end. Quitting Ortus is blocked, including Cmd+Q. You can add 15 minutes. You cannot end it.

  4. 4

    One way out

    End focus early, in Settings, once each calendar week. That reopens what Ortus closed. When a session ends on time, the sites load again and the apps stay closed until you open them.

03 · The agent

Close Slack. Your agent's on it.

Need the decision from Slack, or a file from email? Ask Chat. You never open the app that would eat the morning.

  • Chat runs the Claude Code already on your Mac, with your sign-in and the tools you connected there. Slack, email, calendar, docs.
  • It answers the question and leaves the rest. It will not send or change anything until you tell it to.
  • The blocker doesn't need any of this. No Claude Code, no account, the session still locks.

Connecting Slack in Settings is separate. That only sets your status and Do Not Disturb for the session. Reading Slack is Chat.

04 · Private, and free

Nothing of yours comes to us.

  • No account

    Ortus doesn't sign you up. Settings, modes and the session stay on your Mac.

  • Keychain

    On a signed release, Slack credentials live in the macOS login Keychain. They are not synced to iCloud.

  • Analytics, off

    Anonymous usage events stay off until you opt in. They never include messages, the sites you block, or what you ask in Chat.

  • The extension

    It talks only to the Ortus app on your Mac. It sends nothing over the internet, and it doesn't keep your browsing history.

05 · Before you lock in

The honest part.

What if something urgent happens?

For almost everyone, nothing is so urgent it can't wait until the session ends. The fear is real. The urgency usually isn't. Ask your agent and you get the answer without the rest of the inbox. If it truly can't wait, End focus early is in Settings, once a week. Ortus will not ping you on its own.

Can I get out of a session?

If you started it, you have 30 seconds to press Never mind. A scheduled session has no cancel window. After that there is no end button, and you can't quit Ortus. You can add 15 minutes from the panel. The other way out is End focus early, once each calendar week, which reopens the apps Ortus closed. A normal ending doesn't. The sites come back. The apps stay closed until you open them.

Which browsers does it block?

Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave, through the Chrome Web Store extension. Turn on Allow in Incognito. If the extension is off during a website session, Ortus warns you, counts down 60 seconds, and quits that browser. Safari and Firefox aren't supported. Mac apps close with no browser at all.

Do I need Claude Code?

Not for blocking. Chat is the part that needs it: your own install, your sign-in, your tools. If it isn't on the Mac, sessions still lock.

Is it signed? Which Macs?

Releases are signed and notarized. macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Open the dmg and drag Ortus to Applications. There's also a one-line install from Terminal, in the download window.

What about my phone?

Ortus guards your Mac. It doesn't touch your phone. Put the phone in another room.
