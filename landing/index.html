How it works

Built to keep you in. Not let you out.

  1. 1

    Block from the menu bar

    Ortus blocks your distractions straight from the macOS menu bar. Run it on a schedule, or kick off a session on demand.

  2. 2

    No end button

    Once a session starts, there's no off switch to talk yourself into. The door stays closed until the time is up.

  3. 3

    Chat, not chatter

    Need something while you're heads down? Ask in chat and get just the info you need, none of the distractions.
