Ortus is running

Look for the sun in your menu bar. Every session starts there.

Ortus is hardcore

Once a session starts, it runs to the end. That's the point.

  • No off button. You get a short grace period to cancel a session you just started, then it's locked.
  • No way out. Ortus stays open, blocked apps close again if you reopen them, and turning off the browser extension won't get you out.
  • One emergency exit a week, in Settings, for when something really can't wait.

Block websites too

Ortus closes Mac apps on its own. For websites, add its extension. Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave all install it from the Chrome Web Store.

Add to Chrome

Then open its Details and turn on Allow in Incognito, so private windows are covered too. Safari and Firefox aren't supported yet.

One choice

Save choice

Your browser will ask to open Ortus. You can change it anytime in Ortus → Settings.
