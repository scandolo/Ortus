Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version

  • Ortus has no accounts. It runs on your Mac, and your settings stay there.
  • We never see your messages, the websites you visit, or your AI chats.
  • The browser extension talks only to the Ortus app on your Mac. It sends nothing over the internet.
  • If you opt in, the Mac app sends anonymous usage events, like "focus session started", so we can see what works. It's off unless you turn it on.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or share data with anyone for marketing.

Who we are

Ortus is made by Federico Scandolara, an independent developer based in Italy, who is responsible for the data described here. Questions or requests: support@ortusmode.com.

The Mac app

Stored only on your Mac

  • Your settings, modes, schedules and the state of your current session, in your macOS preferences.
  • If you connect Slack: your Slack sign-in, in a file in ~/Library/Application Support/Ortus that only your Mac user account can read.
  • The list of websites to block during a session, which the browser extension reads.

To remove all of it, delete Ortus, the ~/Library/Application Support/Ortus folder, and ~/Library/Preferences/com.ortusmode.app.plist.

Anonymous usage events

If you opt in, on the welcome page after installing or in Ortus → Settings → Share anonymous usage, the app sends short events to PostHog, our analytics provider, which stores them in the United States. Examples: the app opened, a focus session started, a schedule was added, the weekly emergency end was used, an update was installed, a chat message was sent, Slack was connected.

Each event carries a random ID for your installation (not linked to your name or email), the Ortus and macOS versions, your Mac's model, language and screen size, and a few counts, such as how many days a schedule covers. Like any internet request, it also reveals your IP address, which PostHog can use to estimate a rough location.

Events never include message contents, the websites or apps you block or visit, your Slack data, or what you ask in chat.

Nothing is sent until you opt in, and turning it off in Settings stops it straight away. Earlier versions sent these events by default; email us if you'd like those deleted.

Updates

Ortus checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from it. GitHub receives your IP address as part of those requests, under its privacy statement.

Slack (optional)

If you connect Slack, Ortus uses your sign-in only to set your Slack status and Do Not Disturb when a session starts and ends. It talks directly to Slack; nothing goes through us.

Chat (optional)

Chat runs your own Claude Code on your Mac. Your questions and the answers travel between Claude Code and Anthropic under your own Claude account and Anthropic's terms, and any connected tools (such as Slack or Gmail) are the ones you set up in Claude. Ortus doesn't send your chats to us and doesn't keep them after you quit. Claude Code may keep its own history on your Mac.

The browser extension

The Ortus extension for Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave has one job: block the websites you chose in the Ortus app while a focus session is on.

  • It gets your block list from the Ortus app on your Mac, through the browser's native messaging. It makes no internet requests.
  • To block a site, it compares the address of the pages you open with your block list, on your device. It doesn't record, store or send your browsing history.
  • It stores, in your browser only: whether it's connected to Ortus, a random ID that tells your browser profiles apart, and the current session's rules, which are cleared when the browser closes.
  • It has no analytics and no ads.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

ortusmode.com is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics.

Email

Mail to support@ortusmode.com is forwarded by Cloudflare to our inbox. We use it only to reply to you.

Legal basis and how long we keep data

We send anonymous usage events only with your consent, which you can withdraw anytime in Settings. We keep usage events for up to 24 months, and support emails for as long as needed to help you.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete data about you, or object to its use, by emailing support@ortusmode.com. Because usage events aren't linked to your identity, we may need your installation ID to find them. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Italy that's the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Children

Ortus isn't directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also say so in the app.
